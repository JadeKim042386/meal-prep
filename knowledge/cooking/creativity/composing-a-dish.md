# Composing a Dish

A complete, satisfying plate is rarely one flavor done well — it is a small system of contrasts in balance. This note gives a structural template you can lay over almost any savory dish: **base (protein / starch / veg) + sauce + fat + acid + crunch/texture + aroma/garnish**. Samin Nosrat frames the same idea as four levers — Salt, Fat, Acid, Heat — plus the human craving for *contrast* in flavor, texture, temperature, and color. Page & Dornenburg add the "aesthetic of flavor": taste + mouthfeel + aroma in harmony. Use the template as a checklist: a dish that feels "missing something" almost always has an empty slot.

## Process / Framework
- [principle] Every satisfying plate balances the senses through contrast — flavor, texture, temperature, and color (Nosrat). If everything is soft, beige, and warm, it reads dull no matter how well-seasoned. #contrast
- [technique] The structural slots to fill: **(1) Base** — protein and/or starch and/or vegetable, the bulk and identity. #base
- [technique] **(2) Sauce / moisture** — the connective tissue that unifies the plate (a [[Stocks and Mother Sauces]] derivative, a pan sauce, a dressing, a yogurt swoosh). #sauce
- [technique] **(3) Fat** — carries aroma and creates richness/coating ([[Fat as Flavor Carrier]], [[Fats and Oils]]); butter, oil, cheese, cream, the meat's own fat. #fat
- [technique] **(4) Acid** — the lift that keeps richness from cloying ([[Acidity and Brightness]], [[Acids]]); a squeeze of citrus, a vinegar drizzle, pickles, fresh tomato. #acid
- [technique] **(5) Crunch / textural contrast** — the most-forgotten slot ([[Texture and Mouthfeel]]); toasted nuts/seeds, croutons, fried shallots, crisp skin, raw veg. #texture
- [technique] **(6) Aroma / garnish** — fresh top notes that hit the nose first ([[Herbs]], citrus zest, chili oil, scallion); flavor is mostly aroma (see [[Taste and Aroma Perception]]). #aroma
- [principle] Salt threads through ALL slots, not just the protein (Nosrat: season in layers); season the base, the sauce, AND finish ([[Seasoning and Salting]]). #salt-everywhere
- [principle] Build depth so the dish has a beginning/middle/end on the palate: a savory umami foundation ([[Umami and Synergy]], [[Building Depth and Layering]]) under the bright top notes. #depth
- [decision] Final scan: do I have salt, fat, acid, a crunch, and a fresh aroma? Is there color contrast on the plate? Empty slots → fill them; that is usually the "secret" a dish was missing. #checklist

## How To Apply
Run any plate through the slot checklist and fill gaps:

| Slot | Question | If empty, add… |
|---|---|---|
| Base | What is the bulk + identity? | the protein/starch/veg you started with |
| Sauce | Is it dry / disconnected? | pan sauce, dressing, yogurt, salsa, broth |
| Fat | Does it coat the tongue? | butter, oil, cheese, cream, egg yolk |
| Acid | Does it taste heavy/flat? | citrus, vinegar, pickle, tomato, yogurt |
| Crunch | Is everything soft? | toasted nuts/seeds, fried shallot, crouton, crisp skin |
| Aroma | Does the nose get nothing? | fresh herbs, zest, chili oil, scallion, toasted spice |

**Worked design — a grain bowl from scratch.** Base: farro + roasted sweet potato + kale. It "needs something." Walk the slots: Sauce → a tahini-lemon drizzle (also covers Fat + Acid + Aroma partially). Fat → tahini + olive oil. Acid → lemon in the dressing, plus quick-pickled red onion. Crunch → toasted pepitas + the crispy edges of the roasted sweet potato. Aroma → chopped parsley + a dust of cumin. Salt → in the roast, in the dressing, flaky finish. Result is a balanced plate where each bite has soft + crunchy, rich + bright, earthy + fresh.

## Examples
**Example A — Rescuing a plain pan-seared chicken breast.** Base = chicken (well-seared, [[Maillard and Caramelization]]). It's correct but boring → the plate has only Base + a little Fat. Compose outward: Sauce = quick lemon-butter pan sauce (deglaze, [[Stocks and Mother Sauces]] logic); Acid = the lemon; Fat = butter; Crunch = toasted almonds or crisp capers; Aroma = parsley + lemon zest. Same protein, now a complete dish — purely by filling empty slots.

**Example B — Designing a 비빔밥-style bowl with full slot coverage.** Base = rice + 나물 (seasoned vegetables) + 소고기. Sauce = 고추장 sauce (see [[Korean Flavor Grammar]]). Fat = 참기름 (sesame oil) + a fried egg yolk. Acid = a splash of rice vinegar in the 고추장 sauce, or pickled radish. Crunch = toasted sesame + crisp 누룽지 (rice crust) if you press the rice. Aroma = sesame oil + scallion + 김 (toasted seaweed). Note how the traditional dish already fills every slot — which is *why* it satisfies; the template is reverse-engineered from dishes like this.
