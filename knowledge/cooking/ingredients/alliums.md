# Alliums
마늘, 양파, 대파, shallot, and leek are the savory backbone of most cuisines because they shape-shift with technique: pungent and hot when raw, sweet and mellow when slow-cooked. Understanding the sulfur enzyme switch behind that transformation lets you dial an allium anywhere from a sharp raw bite to caramelized jam, and swap one for another with intent rather than habit.

## Behavior / Mechanism
- [fact] Intact allium cells store odorless sulfoxide precursors (alliin in 마늘, isoalliin in 양파) separated from the enzyme alliinase; cutting/crushing ruptures cells and alliinase converts precursors to pungent thiosulfinates (allicin) within seconds #chemistry
- [technique] Rest crushed 마늘 ~10 min before cooking to let alliinase fully generate allicin; or add it late raw for maximum sharpness #enzyme
- [fact] Alliinase is heat-deactivated — cooking before cell rupture (e.g. roasting whole heads) prevents pungency and yields sweet, mild flavor #heat
- [fact] Finer cut = more cells ruptured = more pungency: a microplaned clove is far hotter than a sliced one #cutting
- [technique] 양파 caramelization is low-and-slow (20-45+ min): water cooks off, then sugars brown via combined Maillard + caramelization into deep sweetness; a pinch of baking soda raises pH and dramatically speeds Maillard browning #maillard
- [fact] 마늘 burns and turns acrid/bitter fast above moderate heat — caramelize gently on low ~20-30 min for sweet golden notes, or it scorches before browning evenly #pitfall
- [fact] Onion's lachrymatory factor (tear gas) forms only after cutting; chilling the 양파 slows the enzyme and reduces crying #science

## Selection
- [fact] Choose 마늘/양파 that are firm and heavy for size with tight dry skins; no green sprouts (bitter), soft spots, or mold #quality
- [fact] Shallots give a refined, slightly sweet garlic-onion hybrid flavor ideal raw in vinaigrettes; 대파 and leek contribute milder, grassy-sweet allium notes #selection
- [fact] Spring/fresh alliums (green garlic, new onions) are milder and sweeter than cured storage bulbs #seasonality

## Creative Application & Substitution
Use the raw-vs-cooked spectrum as a creative lever: the same 양파 can be a sharp pickled garnish, a sweated aromatic base, or a jammy caramelized topping. Substitute within the family by matching intensity — 3:1 shallot:garlic mimics a balanced aromatic base; leek or 대파 whites replace 양파 for a gentler sweetness in stocks and braises. For raw heat without lingering bite, soak sliced 양파 in cold water or quick-pickle it: the acid and water leach pungent thiosulfinates.

## Pitfalls / Fixes
- [fact] Burnt 마늘 is irreversibly bitter — start it in cooler oil or add after other aromatics #fix
- [fact] Bitter/green raw garlic from old cloves: remove the central germ #fix
- [technique] Harsh raw onion in a salsa or salad: rinse, salt, or briefly acid-marinate to tame it #fix
