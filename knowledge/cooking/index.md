# Cooking Knowledge Index

Hub for the principle/technique-centric cooking knowledge base (see [[ADR 0003 Cooking Knowledge Taxonomy]]). The base is built to *generate* novel, delicious recipes, not just store fixed ones: read the **Creativity** layer to invent, and follow its links down into the foundations. 59 notes across 6 domains.

## How to use it for invention
Start at [[Recipe Development Process]] → structure with [[Composing a Dish]] → connect ingredients with [[Flavor Bridging]] → vary an existing dish with [[Riffing and Reverse Engineering]] → diagnose problems with [[Fixing a Dish]]. Each links down into the principles, flavor science, techniques, grammars, and ingredients that justify it.
