# Marination and Tenderizing

Marinating and tenderizing are about manipulating protein structure before heat — to soften tough fibers, hold moisture, and carry flavor inward. There are five distinct mechanisms (enzymatic, acidic, salt, mechanical, dairy) and each works differently, on a different timescale, with different risks. Knowing which lever to pull turns a cheap, tough cut into something tender and deeply seasoned.

## Principle / Mechanism
- [fact] Enzymatic: proteases actively cut protein chains (hydrolysis). 배(pear) & 키위(actinidin), 파인애플(bromelain), papaya(papain), fig(ficin) #enzymatic
- [fact] Enzymes work fast and aggressively — limit to ~30–60 min; longer turns the surface to mush. Most active ~50–70 °C, deactivated by high heat #enzyme-timing
- [technique] 배(Korean pear) is the classic 불고기/갈비 tenderizer — gentle enzyme + sweetness; grate it raw into the marinade #korean-pear
- [fact] Acidic: vinegar, citrus, wine, 막걸리 denature/unwind surface proteins; effective ~pH 3.8–4.5 but only penetrates a few mm and can toughen/dry if too long #acidic
- [fact] Salt (dry brine or brine): denatures proteins so they hold MORE water — boosts juiciness and seasons throughout; the most reliable, lowest-risk method #salt
- [ratio] Wet brine: ~5–6% salt by weight of water (≈ 50–60 g/L); dry brine: ~1% salt by weight of meat, rest uncovered ≥40 min to overnight #brine-ratio
- [fact] Dairy (yogurt, buttermilk): mild lactic acid tenderizes gently + calcium activates the meat's own enzymes (calpains); clings and seasons without toughening — basis of tandoori #dairy
- [technique] Mechanical: pounding, scoring, needling, or grinding physically breaks fibers and connective tissue — instant, no chemistry needed #mechanical
- [fact] Oil in a marinade carries fat-soluble aromatics and promotes browning, but does NOT tenderize #oil

## Why It Works
Meat toughness comes from tightly coiled muscle proteins and collagen connective tissue. Proteases (enzymes) physically cleave peptide bonds, snipping the protein scaffolding — powerful but indiscriminate, which is why over-marinating leaves a pasty, over-soft surface. Acids denature proteins by disrupting the bonds that hold them folded, so they unwind and loosen; but past a point the unwound proteins re-bond tightly and squeeze out water, making meat dry and chalky. Salt works by a different route: at the right concentration it dissolves and denatures muscle proteins (myosin) so the matrix swells and traps water — meat literally holds more moisture after salting, which is why brining is the safest tenderizer. Dairy combines gentle lactic acid with calcium that triggers the meat's native calpain enzymes, giving slow, even tenderizing without the harshness of strong acid.

## Creative Application
Match the mechanism to the cut and the clock. Example 1: 불고기 marinade built on grated 배 (enzyme) + 간장 + 마늘 + 참기름 + a little sugar — the pear tenderizes thin-sliced beef in ~30 min while sugar and 간장 set up Maillard browning on the grill. Example 2: a tandoori-style chicken in yogurt + 고춧가루 + 마늘·생강 overnight — the dairy tenderizes gently and the marinade clings and chars beautifully. For a fast weeknight fix on a thick steak, skip the marinade and DRY-BRINE: salt it, rest uncovered in the fridge, and you get deeper seasoning and a better sear than any acid soak. Never combine enzymatic fruit with dairy or egg marinades — the proteases will curdle casein/albumin.

## Pitfalls / Fixes
- Mushy surface, raw-mealy texture: enzymatic marinade left too long — cap fruit marinades at 30–60 min. #fix
- Dry, chalky meat after a citrus/vinegar marinade: acid over-denatured the surface — shorten time, dilute the acid, add oil/dairy. #fix
- Marinade flavor only skin-deep: most marinades penetrate just a few mm — score the surface, or salt early so seasoning travels in. #fix
- No browning off the grill: too wet / sugary-burnt — pat dry before searing; sugar marinades need moderate heat to avoid scorching. #fix
- Tough lean cut still tough: collagen needs LOW-SLOW moist heat, not marinade — braise it instead. #fix
