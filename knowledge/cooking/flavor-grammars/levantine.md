# Levantine Flavor Grammar
Levantine cooking (Lebanese, Syrian, Palestinian, Jordanian) builds flavor in layers: a charred or substantial base, a creamy tahini or yogurt sauce, a bright acid, a generous scatter of fresh herbs, toasted nuts, and a finishing drizzle of olive oil. Acid is deliberately over-determined — lemon, sumac, and pomegranate molasses each do a different job. Warm spice (allspice + cinnamon) appears in SAVORY dishes, the inverse of the Western dessert role. Garnishes are functional (sweet pops, tart astringency, crunch), not decorative.

## Flavor Base / Aromatics
- [fact] Garlic pounded to a paste with coarse salt is the foundation (basis of toum); raw for cold dips/sauces, a sizzling fry (taqlia: garlic + cilantro) finishes stews #garlic
- [pairing] Bright herb culture: flat-leaf parsley (the #1 ingredient in tabbouleh, ~2 bunches to 1 cup bulgur), mint (fresh + dried, the yogurt partner), cilantro, dill #herbs
- [fact] za'atar the blend = dried Origanum syriacum (wild thyme) + sumac (tang) + toasted sesame + salt; slurried with olive oil for man'oushe and over labneh #zaatar
- [fact] Baharat / seven-spice has no fixed recipe but is identified by heavy allspice + cinnamon, plus black pepper, clove, nutmeg, coriander, cumin #baharat
- [pairing] Allspice + cinnamon in savory dishes is the most characteristic warm-spice axis — backbone of hashweh, kibbeh, musakhan #allspice-cinnamon

## Acid / Fat / Heat / Umami
- [pairing] Acid character map: lemon (sharp/wet, baseline) · sumac (dry/tannic/earthy) · pomegranate molasses (sour-sweet/syrupy, glazing) · verjuice (soft grape-sour) · yogurt/labneh (lactic/creamy); preserved lemon straddles acid and umami #acid
- [pairing] Fat: olive oil first (cooking + finishing drizzle + preservation), tahini (fat AND savory body), samneh (clarified butter), nuts (pine nuts toasted, walnuts structural) #fat
- [fact] Heat is mild by design: Aleppo pepper / pul biber (~10,000 SHU, fruity raisin-like) — flavor is the major note, heat the minor #aleppo-pepper
- [pairing] Umami layered, not a single bomb: tahini, garlic, sumac, dried mint, charred eggplant (over open flame), kishk (fermented bulgur+yogurt), preserved lemon, tomato #umami

## Signature Combinations
- [pairing] Lemon + garlic + olive oil — the universal dressing/marinade base; extreme form is toum (uses neutral oil, not olive) #lemon-garlic-oil
- [pairing] Tahini + lemon + garlic = tarator; also the hummus base (expect the seize — rescue with water) #tarator
- [pairing] Sumac + onion + parsley — musakhan (oil-poached onions, heavy sumac, roast chicken, pine nuts) and fattoush; non-citrus acid balanced by sweet onion + fatty oil #sumac-onion-parsley
- [pairing] Pomegranate molasses + walnut = muhammara — tangy + sweet + smoky + nutty; tartness keeps the walnut richness from going flat #muhammara
- [pairing] Chickpea + tahini + lemon = hummus; eggplant + tahini = mutabbal (charred whole over flame) #hummus-mutabbal

## Creative Application
Tahini sauce: tahini + lemon + water + garlic + salt (~2:1 tahini:lemon). When water first hits, it SEIZES into a stiff paste; keep adding water and whisk until the emulsion inverts to smooth and pourable — the thickening is a transient stage, not a failure. The five-slot logic: charred/substantial base → creamy tahini/yogurt → bright acid → fresh herbs → toasted nuts → finishing olive oil; every plate balances acid + fat + warm spice + fresh herb, with texture contrast mandatory. Riff 1 — "Charred hispi cabbage, sumac-tahini, dukkah, pomegranate": char cabbage hard, pour over lemon-sumac tahini, scatter hazelnut dukkah, pomegranate seeds, mint, finishing oil. Riff 2 — "Whipped labneh, burnt-butter pine nuts, pomegranate molasses, za'atar" — showing the acid slot is functional, not lemon-specific.

## Pitfalls / Fixes
- Bitter tahini (over-roasted/oxidized seeds): use fresh good tahini; add acid (lemon reduces perceived bitterness), salt, a touch of sweetness; thin with ice water and re-blend.
- Too much lemon: add fat (oil/yogurt), a touch of sweetness, salt, or dilute.
- Raw garlic harshness: steep minced garlic in lemon juice 10-15 min (acid inhibits the enzyme that makes harsh sulfur compounds — Solomonov's hummus method); pound with salt; remove the green germ.
- Tahini seizing: expected — keep adding ice water ~2 Tbsp at a time and whisk until it relaxes.
- Yogurt splitting when heated: stabilize with cornstarch and/or egg whisked in cold, use full-fat, gentle simmer never a hard boil (jameed, long-fermented, is heat-stable — mansaf).
