# Thai Flavor Grammar

Thai cooking thinks in dynamic balance: every dish tunes sweet, sour, salty, and spicy (with bitter and umami as undertones) until no single taste wins — a harmony Thais call ปรับรส (adjusting taste / รสชาติ). Flavor is built from a pounded paste of fresh aromatics and dialed in at the end with the four-corner seasonings. The result is bright, layered, and aromatic rather than long-cooked-deep.

## Flavor Base / Aromatics
- [fact] The flavor frame is the balance of 4–5 tastes: หวาน (sweet) / เปรี้ยว (sour) / เค็ม (salty) / เผ็ด (spicy), with bitter + umami beneath #balance
- [pairing] The aromatic base is a pounded paste (พริกแกง / nam prik): lemongrass + galangal (ข่า) + kaffir lime (ใบมะกรูด) + garlic + shallot + chili + coriander root + กะปิ (shrimp paste) #currypaste
- [pairing] Fresh herbs finish the dish: Thai basil (โหระพา), holy basil (กะเพรา), cilantro, mint, sawtooth coriander #herbs
- [fact] Aromatics are pounded in a mortar (ครก), not just chopped — bruising releases far more aroma than blending #mortar

## Acid / Fat / Heat / Umami
- [pairing] Acid (เปรี้ยว): มะนาว (lime, added off-heat for brightness) and มะขาม (tamarind, for cooked sour depth) #acid
- [pairing] Fat: coconut milk (กะทิ, also body + sweetness) and the cracked coconut cream used to fry curry paste #fat
- [pairing] Heat (เผ็ด): พริกขี้หนู (bird's-eye chili), dried chilies in pastes, พริกป่น (chili flakes), Nam Prik Pao (roasted chili jam) #heat
- [pairing] Salty/Umami: น้ำปลา (fish sauce, primary salt+umami), กะปิ (shrimp paste), soy for some dishes, ปลาร้า (fermented fish) in Isan #umami
- [pairing] Sweet: น้ำตาลปี๊บ (palm sugar) — rounds and bonds the sour and salty edges #sweet

## Signature Combinations
- [pairing] น้ำปลา + มะนาว + น้ำตาล + พริก = the universal dressing axis (ส้มตำ, ยำ salads, dipping sauces) #fourcorner
- [pairing] กะทิ + พริกแกง + น้ำปลา + น้ำตาลปี๊บ + ใบมะกรูด = coconut curry base (แกงเขียวหวาน, แกงเผ็ด) #coconutcurry
- [pairing] กะเพรา + garlic + chili + fish sauce + oyster/soy = ผัดกะพรา (holy-basil stir-fry) #padkrapow
- [pairing] มะขาม + น้ำตาลปี๊บ + น้ำปลา = ผัดไทย sweet-sour-salty sauce #padthai
- [pairing] Nam Prik Pao + lime + fish sauce + chili = ต้มยำ sour-spicy soup backbone #tomyum

## Creative Application
Compose a Thai-feeling dish by first pounding (or using) an aromatic paste, then deciding which of the four tastes will lead and letting the other three support and balance it. Always taste and adjust at the end: too sharp? add palm sugar; too flat? add lime and fish sauce; too salty? more lime + sugar. Finish with fresh herbs off-heat. Example riffs: a roasted-squash green curry where coconut + green paste carry sweetness against fish sauce salt and lime brightness; a 'yam'-style warm mushroom salad dressed in fish sauce, lime, palm sugar, chili, and torn mint. Brightness goes in last.

## Pitfalls / Fixes
- Cooking lime juice: heat dulls its perfume — add lime off the heat, just before serving.
- Only sweet + spicy: without the sour and salty corners it tastes like candy with heat, not Thai.
- Blending the curry paste smooth in a processor: a mortar bruises and releases aroma the blade only purées; processor pastes taste flatter.
- Skipping fish sauce / shrimp paste to make it 'cleaner': you lose the savory funk that grounds the brightness.
- Under-frying curry paste in coconut cream: paste must be fried until oil separates and it smells fragrant, or the curry tastes raw.
